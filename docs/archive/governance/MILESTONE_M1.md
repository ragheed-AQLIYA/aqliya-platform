## 1. Purpose

M1 is a lightweight governance checkpoint between Sprint v1 (Knowledge Governance Foundation) and Sprint v2 (Evidence Authority). Its purpose is to formally accept Sprint v1's outputs and establish the rules for all subsequent work.

M1 is not a delivery review. It is a **contract acceptance** — agreeing that the foundation is solid enough to build upon.

## 3. M1 Acceptance Declaration

All 10 items accepted by Project Owner on 2026-06-29.

```text
I, Project Owner, confirm that:

1. Sprint v1 outputs are accepted as the factual foundation for all governance work.
2. Wave 3A changes are approved and may be committed.
3. Wave 3B changes remain frozen until Governance Review (Sprint v3).
4. The Four Dimensions model (Implementation Reality, Product Maturity, Commercial Claim, Strategic Intent) governs all maturity claims.
5. Assessment Confidence (High/Medium/Low) is required for every claim.
6. Evidence Manifest Rule: No L-level may change without an Evidence Manifest.
7. Evidence Freshness is tracked in every manifest (Evidence Date, Commit, Verification Date, Reviewer, Expires).
8. The Governance Review follows three-tier separation (Producer → Reviewer → Decision Authority).
9. Sprint M2 (Knowledge Data Model) may proceed immediately. Sprint v2 follows after M2.
10. No new engineering program will start before the governance path completes.

Date: 2026-06-29
Decision: M1 accepted — Sprint v2 authorized
```
