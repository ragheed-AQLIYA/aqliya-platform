## Governance Rules

1. **Every new product** starts from the Engineering Standard — not from scratch.
2. **Any deviation from the Standard** requires documented evidence and Architecture Board approval.
3. **No baseline modification** without execution evidence from at least two independent products.
4. **ERR is the mandatory gate** between specification and implementation for every work package.
5. **Architecture Drift Reviews** are required after every work package.
