# AuditOS — Pilot Brief

**Status:** Ready | **Platform:** `app.aqliya.com/audit`

---

## What AuditOS Does Today

AuditOS is a governed financial audit intelligence system that enables:

- **Engagement management** — create, track, and manage audit engagements
- **Evidence vault** — upload, version, and secure audit evidence (S3 + ClamAV)
- **Financial statements** — trial balance, mapping, statements, disclosure notes
- **Review & approval** — structured review workflow with audit trail
- **Governance** — ISQM1 quality, independence checks, client acceptance
- **Exports** — bilingual PDF (Arabic/English) with full audit trail
- **AI assistance** — AI-powered review with human-in-loop governance

## Who It Is For

- Audit firms
- Internal audit departments
- Regulated financial environments requiring evidence governance
