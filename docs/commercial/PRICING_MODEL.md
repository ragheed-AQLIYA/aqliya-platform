# AQLIYA Pricing Model

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-06-30

## Philosophy
AQLIYA is not sold as individual products but as a platform subscription with capability tiers.

## Tiers

### Tier 1: Foundation
- Intelligence Core access
- 1 product workspace (AuditOS XOR LocalContentOS)
- Up to 5 users
- Cloud-hosted
- **SAR 15,000/month**

### Tier 2: Professional
- Intelligence Core + All products
- Up to 25 users
- Cloud-hosted + Dedicated DB
- Priority support
- **SAR 45,000/month**

### Tier 3: Enterprise
- Full platform access
- Unlimited users
- Private cloud option
- Custom integrations
- SLA guarantee
- Dedicated support engineer
- **SAR 120,000/month**

## Add-ons
- On-Prem deployment: +50%
- AI Agent pack (per agent): SAR 5,000/month
- Additional workspace: SAR 10,000/month
- Training & onboarding: SAR 25,000 one-time

## Pilot Pricing
- First 3 months: 50% discount
- Includes dedicated onboarding engineer
- Money-back guarantee after first month

## Notes
- All prices in SAR, exclusive of VAT
- Annual contracts receive 2 months free
- SOC2 certification target: Q4 2026
