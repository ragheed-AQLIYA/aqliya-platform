# AQLIYA B2B Sales Process

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-06-30

## Overview
Enterprise B2B sales process targeting government, semi-government, and regulated private sector entities in Saudi Arabia.

## Sales Stages

### Stage 1: Lead Qualification
- Inbound/outbound lead capture
- Fit assessment: governed intelligence need
- Budget validation
- Decision-maker identification
- **Exit criteria:** Qualified lead with confirmed need and budget

### Stage 2: Discovery & Demo
- Stakeholder mapping
- Deep-dive needs analysis
- Tailored product demo
- Technical feasibility check
- **Exit criteria:** Technical win + stakeholder buy-in

### Stage 3: Proof of Value (Pilot)
- Pilot scope agreement
- Environment provisioning
- Data migration (sample)
- User training
- 2-4 week controlled pilot
- Success criteria: documented in pilot charter
- **Exit criteria:** Pilot success sign-off

### Stage 4: Commercial Proposal
- Pricing proposal (see PRICING_MODEL.md)
- SLA draft
- Implementation timeline
- ROI summary
- **Exit criteria:** Proposal submitted

### Stage 5: Negotiation & Legal
- Contract review
- Pricing negotiation
- Legal/Sharia compliance review
- Security questionnaire
- **Exit criteria:** Signed agreement

### Stage 6: Onboarding & Deployment
- Project kickoff
- Production environment setup
- Full data migration
- User training (phase 2)
- Go-live
- **Exit criteria:** Active usage + first invoice

### Stage 7: Account Management
- Quarterly business reviews
- Product adoption tracking
- Additional product expansion
- Renewal management
- Reference case development

## Key Metrics
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Lead-to-pilot conversion | >30% |
| Pilot-to-sign conversion | >70% |
| Average deal cycle | 6-10 weeks |
| Net revenue retention | >120% |

## Sales Collateral
- Pitch deck (Arabic + English)
- Product demo environment
- Case studies (see docs/assets/)
- Pricing sheet
- Security & compliance pack
- Trial balance / demo data sets
